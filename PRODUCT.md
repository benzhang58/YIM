# GymBusy product outline

## Positioning

GymBusy helps people avoid crowded workouts by combining live crowd reports, historical peak-time intelligence, and gym-specific quality signals.

## MVP features

1. Search gyms by name or area.
2. See a live crowd score with recent member submissions.
3. View expected peak periods by day and time.
4. Read quick reviews about machine quality, cleanliness, and wait times.
5. Submit a fast crowd check-in with one tap.
6. Submit a missing gym when a location is not listed.

## V2 features

1. Follow favorite gyms.
2. Photo posts and short social updates.
3. Machine-specific occupancy reporting.
4. Waitlist or alert when the gym drops below a threshold.
5. Verified gym owner dashboards.

## Launch concerns

- Anti-spam protection on crowd submissions
- Rate limiting and device fingerprinting
- Moderation flow for reviews and photos
- Duplicate detection for new gym submissions
- Privacy policy and account deletion flow
- Terms around user-generated content
