## Full Description

GymBusy helps you choose when and where to train by combining live crowd reports, member reviews, and historical busyness trends.

Use GymBusy to:

- Check live busyness before leaving for the gym.
- Compare nearby gyms by crowd level, distance, ratings, and best training window.
- Report how busy your gym is in one tap.
- Review equipment quality, cleanliness, spacing, and wait times.
- Submit missing gyms for moderation when your location is not listed yet.
- Flag misleading reviews or suspicious crowd reports for review.

GymBusy is built for people who want a smoother workout without guessing when the gym will be packed.
